## Method and acceptance criteria

The audit traced source prescriptions through four layers: phase templates, restrictions and historical decisions, active workout logging, and the browser interface/persistence. Each discovered defect received a reproduction and a regression check. Existing tests were extended where they had asserted incomplete behavior, including the cut/fly observation window and heavy-slot effort cap.

Completion requires every finding below to be resolved, the source checklist reviewed, and `npm run audit` to pass on the final files. The audit does not count a passing test of a simple case as coverage of its recovery, event, checkpoint or interruption variants.

Source observations remain explicit human inputs: actual readiness, safe technique/release, competent protection, comparable video/conditions, two green weeks, and whether a trial helps the priority lifts. The app records these observations and restricts the resulting prescription. It does not invent a physiological clearance score, a diagnosis from ratios, or evidence that missing observations occurred. Recorded contrary history blocks the relevant progression. Existing-training exceptions use the documented reviewed-start workflow.

## Time-estimate revision

The subsequent time-planning change replaces the generic per-set estimate with exercise-specific execution, specific ramps, work-set rests, equipment transitions and waits, visit overhead, breaks, field preparation, scheduled mobility and separate inter-visit gaps. Settings are validated and frozen for active sessions; they do not affect prescription doses or progression. Assumptions and limits are documented in [TIME-ESTIMATES.md](TIME-ESTIMATES.md). Ten additional regression tests and a fourth browser suite cover this revision. These planning ranges have not been calibrated against measured personal workout durations.

## Executable preparation and mobility revision

The previous audit did not verify that mobility could run as a session; it was display-only. Selected stretches now have an executable session, timed holds/rests, active-rep completion, reload/offline recovery and complete or partial journal records. General, field, specific and interruption warm-ups have resumable timers and completion records. Mobility time is counted once in the day/session estimates, including all 52 program weeks. A waiting-update banner addresses stale cached clients, and its activation is tested for journal preservation and active-session protection. Six additional unit tests and two further browser suites cover these paths. The provided local tab was observed on the old estimates before its update was activated.

## Aerobic runner and update follow-through

Aerobics previously had manual logging without an executable moving-time clock. It now provides start, pause, resume and actual-minute confirmation for both main sessions and additional walks. Pauses are excluded, targets remain frozen, shorter bouts are partial, and stopped unconfirmed timers retain their trace. The easy start is part of moving time; no separate lifting warm-up is invented. Tests execute cardio and mobility on the same non-lifting day through offline reload. Three additional unit regressions cover timing, shortened/stopped work and readiness/import validation. The browser suite also verifies saved mobility creates Start buttons and nonzero budgets on Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday.

App 7.12 shows its installed build in Settings and on desktop, checks for updates on focus/reconnection, and offers an explicit update check. Activation checks the saved active session as well as the local tab, so a stale tab cannot activate an update over another tab’s workout. Tests preserve the journal through activation.

## Reproduction

Run `npm ci`, then `npm run audit`. Chrome is used by default; `OLY_BROWSER_CHANNEL=chromium` selects an installed Playwright Chromium. Browser artifacts are written to ignored `test-results/`. The new service-worker cache is `oly-groundup-v7-12`; an existing workout is not replaced mid-session. Use Settings → Check for updates and the update banner after saving form changes and finishing active work. A version predating the banner must close all app tabs before reopening; a normal reload may retain the previous offline worker.
